About Sarathi

A Construction Company Built On Experience

Sarathi Construction Pvt. Ltd.

Established in 2018, Sarathi Construction Pvt. Ltd. is a Kathmandu-based construction company working across residential, commercial and infrastructure projects.

Over the years, we have completed more than 15 projects, each bringing its own requirements, challenges and working environment. That experience has taught us the importance of planning properly, coordinating closely and paying attention to the details that matter on site.

We work with clients from the early planning stage through construction and completion, keeping communication practical and straightforward along the way.

What We Do

Construction Services That Make Sense

From planning and design to construction management, we focus on practical solutions that suit the project, the site and the client's requirements.

Construction Management

We coordinate the different stages of construction, from site activities and contractors to quality checks and project progress. The goal is simple: keep the work organized and moving in the right direction.

Planning & Consulting

Good construction starts with a good plan. We help with project planning, feasibility, cost considerations and resource coordination before work begins on site.

Architecture & Design

We work toward designs that balance appearance, functionality and practical construction requirements, helping clients make better decisions before the work begins.

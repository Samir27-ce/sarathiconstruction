About Sarathi

A Construction Company Built On Experience

Sarathi Construction Pvt. Ltd.

We started Sarathi Construction in 2018 out of Tokha, and most of our early jobs were private homes in the neighbourhood — the kind where the client is checking on the foundation work every other day. That habit of staying close to the site never really left us, even as the projects got bigger.

Since then we've built a branch office for Muktinath Bikas Bank in Lazimpat, the Omoda Jaecoo showroom, government buildings including a Chandragiri municipality structure and work for the Office of the Auditor General, alongside a steady run of houses in Tokha and Sanepa. Fifteen-plus sites in, we've learned that most construction problems come from poor coordination, not bad workmanship — so that's where we put the effort.

We sit with clients before the first brick goes down, walk through the site plan, and stay reachable through every stage after that. If something on site needs a decision — a design change, a material substitution, a delay from the monsoon — you hear about it directly from us, not through three layers of subcontractors.

What We Do

What We Actually Do On Site

Some clients come to us with drawings already done and just need someone to build it right. Others start with an empty plot and a rough idea. Either way works — here's where we fit in.

Construction Management

Someone from our team is on your site daily — not just for pours or handovers. We schedule the masons, plumbers and electricians so they aren't tripping over each other, catch rebar and finishing issues before they're plastered over, and give you a straight answer when a deadline is slipping.

Planning & Consulting

Before we quote anything, we walk the plot with you, check soil and access conditions, and work out a realistic budget and timeline — the kind that accounts for monsoon delays and municipality approval waits, not the kind that looks good on paper and falls apart in month two.

Architecture & Design

We work with architects on layout and elevation, but we're also the ones who'll tell you if a design detail is going to be a headache to build or push your budget up — before it's poured in concrete instead of drawn on paper.
